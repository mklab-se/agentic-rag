# Voltrieve service guide

This guide describes what each workshop job consists of on every Voltrieve model. It names the components a job needs. Article numbers are not listed here: they are in the spare parts catalog.

## Before any work under the car

Put the car in service mode from the centre screen. This releases the electric parking brake, stops regenerative braking and keeps the high voltage system asleep. Never open orange high voltage cables. Left and right are seen from the driver's seat.

| Model | Front discs | Front caliper | Rear discs | Rear caliper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voltrieve Spark | 280 mm on RWD | single piston floating | 265 mm | single piston floating |
| Voltrieve Lumen | 300 mm on RWD, 320 mm on AWD | twin piston floating | 290 mm | single piston floating |
| Voltrieve Ragn | 350 mm on AWD | twin piston floating | 330 mm | single piston floating |

| Voltrieve Atlas | 400 mm on AWD | six piston fixed | 360 mm | twin piston floating |

## Complete front brake replacement on one side

A complete front brake replacement renews every wearing and hydraulic part of the brake on one corner of the car. Pads are always renewed as an axle set, even when only one side is being worked on. The other side of the axle keeps its disc and caliper unless they are worn too.

### Voltrieve Spark: complete front brake replacement on one side

Applies to the Voltrieve Spark (RWD). The job needs:

- 1 brake disc for the side being worked on
- 1 brake pad set for the front axle. The set covers both sides of the axle
- 1 brake caliper for the side being worked on
- 1 caliper carrier for the side being worked on
- 1 brake hose for the side being worked on
- 1 caliper fitting kit for the front axle
- 1 brake dust shield for the side being worked on
- 1 bottle of brake fluid DOT 4 LV, for bleeding the circuit afterwards

The Voltrieve Spark has no electrical pad wear sensor.

### Voltrieve Lumen: complete front brake replacement on one side

Applies to the Voltrieve Lumen (RWD and AWD). The job needs:

## Wheel bearing replacement

The wheel bearing and hub is one sealed unit. Renew it per corner. The job needs 1 wheel bearing and hub for that corner, and the brake disc must come off to reach it. On AWD cars the front drive shaft nut is renewed at the same time.

## Cabin air filter

The cabin air filter sits behind the passenger footwell trim and is changed every two years. No tools are needed apart from a trim tool.

## Air suspension on the Voltrieve Fjord

The Voltrieve Fjord has air suspension. Before lifting the car, turn off automatic levelling from the centre screen. A front air strut or rear air spring is renewed one corner at a time.
