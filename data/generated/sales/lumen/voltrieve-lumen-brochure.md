## Meet the Lumen

The Lumen is a compact hatchback with the reflexes of a sports car and the manners of a daily driver. Low, light and eager, it makes an ordinary commute feel like the long way home.

The Voltrieve Lumen is a hatchback with rear wheel drive or all wheel drive, built on the Voltrieve V1 platform with a 400 V system. Light on its feet: that is the idea behind every detail.

## Technology and Voltrieve Pilot

The Voltrieve Lumen comes with Voltrieve Pilot version 2, our driver assistance suite. It brings adaptive cruise and lane keeping.
Voltrieve Pilot Plus adds lane change assist and the highway autopilot. It is included on Lumen Prime and available as an option pack on the other trims.

- Frunk of 45 litres
- 11 kW onboard charger
- 360 camera
- A heat pump as standard, so the range holds up in winter

## Range and charging

With a 61 kWh battery the Voltrieve Lumen goes up to 455 km on a charge (WLTP). At a fast charger it takes up to 135 kW and goes from 10 to 80 % in about 26 minutes. At home, the 11 kW onboard charger fills the battery overnight.

| Battery | Range (WLTP) | Peak DC charging | 10 to 80 % | Onboard AC charger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 61 kWh | 455 km | 135 kW | 26 min | 11 kW |

## Questions and answers

**How far can the Lumen go on a charge?**
Up to 455 km (WLTP) with the 61 kWh battery. Cold weather, high speeds and a full load shorten that.

**How fast does it charge?**
From 10 to 80 % in about 26 minutes at a fast charger that delivers 135 kW or more. On AC it charges at up to 11 kW, empty to full in about 5.5 hours.

**Can it tow?**
Yes, up to 750 kg braked.

**Does it have a heat pump?**
Yes, on every trim.

**Which driver assistance does it have?**
Voltrieve Pilot version 2: adaptive cruise and lane keeping.
