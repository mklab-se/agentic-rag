## What each Lumen trim adds

### Lumen Pure

- Heat pump
- Double LED light lines front and rear
- 360 camera
- 11 kW onboard charger
- 12.3 inch driver display and floating touchscreen
- Voltrieve Pilot 2 with adaptive cruise control
- Frunk of 45 litres
- 18 inch Arc wheels, Black with coral stitching interior

### Lumen Plus

Everything in Lumen Pure, plus:

- Power tailgate
- Keyless entry
- Head-up display
- 19 inch Turbine wheels, Black with coral stitching interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning

### Lumen Prime

Everything in Lumen Plus, plus:

- Adaptive high beam
- Heated windscreen
- Ambient lighting in 12 colors
- 19 inch Turbine wheels, Sport seats interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning
- Pilot Plus pack included: Voltrieve Pilot lane change assist and highway autopilot upgrade
- Sound pack included: 14 speaker Voltrieve Stage audio system

|---|---|

| Effective rate | 5.43 % |
| Setup fee | 695 SEK |
| Total amount payable | 451 767 SEK |

Example in Sweden: cash price 409 000 SEK, deposit 81 800 SEK, loan 327 200 SEK over 36 months at a nominal rate of 4.95 % (effective rate 5.43 %). The monthly payment includes an administration fee of 59 SEK. A setup fee of 695 SEK is charged at the start, and a residual payment of 204 500 SEK is due at the end of the term, when you can pay it, refinance it or return the car. Total amount payable 451 767 SEK. Credit is subject to approval. The rate is variable and the example is for illustration only.
