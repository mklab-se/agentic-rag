# Voltrieve Atlas price list

Prices valid from 1 November 2026. Recommended retail prices including VAT, in Sweden (SEK) and in the EU (EUR).

## Atlas prices per trim and drive

| Model | Drive | Price in Sweden | Price in the EU |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atlas Pure | AWD | 749 000 SEK | 67 900 EUR |
| Atlas Plus | AWD | 809 000 SEK | 73 300 EUR |
| Atlas Prime | AWD | 869 000 SEK | 78 800 EUR |

The Voltrieve Atlas has AWD as standard on every trim.

## What each Atlas trim adds

### Atlas Pure

- Heat pump
- Seven seats in three rows
- Rear wheel steering
- Air suspension
- 3.5 kW vehicle to load outlet
- Voltrieve Pilot 3
- Bracket LED lights front and rear
- 21 inch Monolith wheels, Charcoal textile and microsuede interior

### Atlas Plus

Everything in Atlas Pure, plus:

- Power tailgate with hands-free opening
- Three-zone climate
- Head-up display
- 22 inch Monolith wheels, Charcoal textile and microsuede interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning

### Atlas Prime

Everything in Atlas Plus, plus:

- Panoramic glass roof
- Heated second row
- 22 inch Monolith wheels, Cognac Nappa leather interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning
- Pilot Plus pack included: Voltrieve Pilot lane change assist and highway autopilot upgrade
- Sound pack included: 14 speaker Voltrieve Stage audio system

## Atlas financing example

### Financing in Sweden

A Atlas Plus AWD at 809 000 SEK costs **8 996 SEK per month** over 36 months with Voltrieve Financial Services.

| Atlas Plus AWD financing in Sweden | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash price | 809 000 SEK |
| Deposit (20 %) | 161 800 SEK |
| Loan amount | 647 200 SEK |
| Monthly payment for 36 months | 8 996 SEK per month |
| Residual payment at the end (50 %) | 404 500 SEK |
| Nominal rate | 4.95 % |
| Effective rate | 5.25 % |
| Setup fee | 695 SEK |
| Total amount payable | 890 851 SEK |

Example in Sweden: cash price 809 000 SEK, deposit 161 800 SEK, loan 647 200 SEK over 36 months at a nominal rate of 4.95 % (effective rate 5.25 %). The monthly payment includes an administration fee of 59 SEK. A setup fee of 695 SEK is charged at the start, and a residual payment of 404 500 SEK is due at the end of the term, when you can pay it, refinance it or return the car. Total amount payable 890 851 SEK. Credit is subject to approval. The rate is variable and the example is for illustration only.
