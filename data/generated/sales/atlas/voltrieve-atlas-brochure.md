## Meet the Atlas

The Atlas is a seven-seat SUV for big families and big plans. It carries seven people in three rows, tows a large caravan and still charges faster than any other Voltrieve.

The Voltrieve Atlas is a large SUV with all wheel drive, built on the Voltrieve V2 platform with an 800 V system. Seven seats, zero compromise: that is the idea behind every detail.

## Technology and Voltrieve Pilot

The Voltrieve Atlas comes with Voltrieve Pilot version 3, our driver assistance suite. It brings adaptive cruise, lane keeping, traffic jam assist and automatic lane change (with Pilot Plus).
Voltrieve Pilot Plus adds lane change assist and the highway autopilot. It is included on Atlas Prime and available as an option pack on the other trims.

## Range and charging

With a 112 kWh battery the Voltrieve Atlas goes up to 610 km on a charge (WLTP). At a fast charger it takes up to 320 kW and goes from 10 to 80 % in about 20 minutes. At home, the 22 kW onboard charger fills the battery overnight.

| Battery | Range (WLTP) | Peak DC charging | 10 to 80 % | Onboard AC charger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 112 kWh | 610 km | 320 kW | 20 min | 22 kW |

Everything in Atlas Pure, plus:

Everything in Atlas Plus, plus:

- Panoramic glass roof

## Questions and answers

**How far can the Atlas go on a charge?**
Up to 610 km (WLTP) with the 112 kWh battery. Cold weather, high speeds and a full load shorten that.

**How fast does it charge?**
From 10 to 80 % in about 20 minutes at a fast charger that delivers 320 kW or more. On AC it charges at up to 22 kW, empty to full in about 5.1 hours.

**Can it tow?**
Yes, up to 2 500 kg braked. The Tow pack adds an electrically deployable tow bar and trailer stability control.

**Does it have a heat pump?**
Yes, on every trim.

**Which driver assistance does it have?**
Voltrieve Pilot version 3: adaptive cruise, lane keeping, traffic jam assist and automatic lane change (with Pilot Plus).
