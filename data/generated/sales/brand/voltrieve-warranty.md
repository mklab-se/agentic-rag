# Voltrieve warranty and service plans

Every new Voltrieve comes with the same warranty, whichever model you choose: the Voltrieve Spark, Voltrieve Lumen, Voltrieve Ragn, Voltrieve Fjord and Voltrieve Atlas.

## Voltrieve warranty at a glance

| Voltrieve warranty | Cover |
|---|---|
| New car warranty | 3 years or 100 000 km, whichever comes first |
| Battery warranty | 8 years or 160 000 km, whichever comes first, with at least 70 % of the original usable capacity |
| Paint warranty | 3 years |
| Rust perforation warranty | 12 years |
| Roadside assistance | 3 years, renewed at each scheduled service |

## What the Voltrieve warranty covers

- The new car warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for the whole car.
- The battery warranty covers the high voltage battery, its modules and its control units. A battery that falls below 70 % of its original usable capacity within the period is repaired or replaced at no cost.
- Roadside assistance is renewed for one year at each scheduled service at an authorised Voltrieve workshop, for as long as the car is serviced there.
- Wear items such as tyres, brake pads, brake discs and wiper blades are not covered, and neither is damage from accidents, misuse or missing maintenance.
- The warranty follows the car when it changes owner.

## Voltrieve service intervals

Every Voltrieve is serviced every 24 months or 30 000 km, whichever comes first. The car tells you when a service is due and books it with your workshop if you want it to. Software updates arrive over the air between services.

## Voltrieve service plans

A service plan spreads the cost of servicing into a fixed monthly amount.

| Service plan | Period | What it includes | Price in Sweden | Price in the EU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voltrieve Care | 3 years or 60 000 km | scheduled services, brake fluid change, cabin air filter, wiper blades, software updates at the workshop | 249 SEK per month | 24 EUR per month |
| Voltrieve Care Plus | 5 years or 100 000 km | everything in Voltrieve Care, brake pads and discs, 12 V battery, seasonal tyre change and storage, a courtesy car at each service | 549 SEK per month | 52 EUR per month |

Service plans can be added when you buy the car or at any time during the first year. They apply to every Voltrieve model.

## Voltrieve roadside assistance

If your Voltrieve stops on the road, for any reason including a flat battery or a flat tyre, call Voltrieve Assistance any time of day. We send help, tow the car to the nearest authorised Voltrieve workshop and arrange onward travel if the repair takes longer than the same day. Roadside assistance is included for 3 years and renewed for one year at each scheduled service.
