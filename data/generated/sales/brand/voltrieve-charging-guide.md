# Voltrieve charging guide

How to charge a Voltrieve at home and on the road, what it costs, and how to get the most out of the battery in winter.

## Charging a Voltrieve at home

Most charging happens at home, overnight. The Voltrieve Home Charger (article number VA-9001) is a 22 kW wall box with a type 2 cable and app control. It costs 11 900 SEK or 1 090 EUR; installation by an electrician is extra. The car charges at the lower of the wall box rating and its own onboard charger.

| Model | Battery | Onboard AC charger | Empty to full on a home charger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voltrieve Spark | 42 kWh | 11 kW | about 3.8 hours |
| Voltrieve Lumen | 61 kWh | 11 kW | about 5.5 hours |
| Voltrieve Ragn | 84 kWh | 22 kW | about 3.8 hours |
| Voltrieve Fjord | 84 kWh | 22 kW | about 3.8 hours |
| Voltrieve Atlas | 112 kWh | 22 kW | about 5.1 hours |

The portable charger with a household plug is for occasional use, such as a night at a summer cottage. It charges much more slowly and should not be used every day.

## Charging a Voltrieve on the road

At a public AC charger, use the mode 3 type 2 cable. At a fast DC charger the cable is fixed to the charger. Every Voltrieve has a CCS2 charge port.

| Model | Peak DC charging | 10 to 80 % at a fast charger |
|---|---|---|
| Voltrieve Spark | 95 kW | about 25 minutes |
| Voltrieve Lumen | 135 kW | about 26 minutes |
| Voltrieve Ragn | 240 kW | about 21 minutes |
| Voltrieve Fjord | 240 kW | about 20 minutes |
| Voltrieve Atlas | 320 kW | about 20 minutes |

Charging slows down above 80 %, so on a long trip it is usually quicker to stop twice for 20 minutes than once for an hour. The route planner in the car picks the chargers and preconditions the battery on the way.

## Charging a Voltrieve in winter

- **Precondition before you leave.** Set a departure time in the app and the car warms the cabin and the battery while it is still plugged in, so the energy comes from the grid and not from the battery.
- **Precondition before a fast charge.** Choose the charger in the route planner and the car warms the battery on the way. A cold battery charges much more slowly.
- **Use the heat pump.** Voltrieve Lumen, Voltrieve Ragn, Voltrieve Fjord and Voltrieve Atlas have a heat pump as standard. The Voltrieve Spark gets one with the Winter pack, standard on Spark Plus and Prime.
- **Expect less range.** In real winter conditions the range is often 20 to 30 % below the WLTP figure. Seat and steering wheel heating use less energy than heating the whole cabin.
- **Park inside when you can.** A battery that stays above freezing keeps more of its range and charges faster.
- **Charge to 80 % every day** and to 100 % only before a long trip.
