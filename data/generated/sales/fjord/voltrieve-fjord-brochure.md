## Meet the Fjord

The Fjord is a long, low estate for families who would rather drive than fly. It covers great distances in quiet comfort and swallows everything the weekend needs.

The Voltrieve Fjord is an estate with rear wheel drive or all wheel drive, built on the Voltrieve V2 platform with an 800 V system. Room for the whole weekend: that is the idea behind every detail.

## Technology and Voltrieve Pilot

The Voltrieve Fjord comes with Voltrieve Pilot version 3, our driver assistance suite. It brings adaptive cruise, lane keeping, traffic jam assist and automatic lane change (with Pilot Plus).
Voltrieve Pilot Plus adds lane change assist and the highway autopilot. It is included on Fjord Prime and available as an option pack on the other trims.

- Boot of 640 litres

## Range and charging

With a 84 kWh battery the Voltrieve Fjord goes up to 575 km on a charge (WLTP). At a fast charger it takes up to 240 kW and goes from 10 to 80 % in about 20 minutes. At home, the 22 kW onboard charger fills the battery overnight.

| Battery | Range (WLTP) | Peak DC charging | 10 to 80 % | Onboard AC charger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 84 kWh | 575 km | 240 kW | 20 min | 22 kW |

## Questions and answers

**How far can the Fjord go on a charge?**
Up to 575 km (WLTP) with the 84 kWh battery. Cold weather, high speeds and a full load shorten that.

**How fast does it charge?**
From 10 to 80 % in about 20 minutes at a fast charger that delivers 240 kW or more. On AC it charges at up to 22 kW, empty to full in about 3.8 hours.

**Can it tow?**
Yes, up to 2 100 kg braked. The Tow pack adds an electrically deployable tow bar and trailer stability control.

**Does it have a heat pump?**
Yes, on every trim.

**Which driver assistance does it have?**
Voltrieve Pilot version 3: adaptive cruise, lane keeping, traffic jam assist and automatic lane change (with Pilot Plus).
