## What each Fjord trim adds

### Fjord Pure

- Heat pump
- Air suspension
- LED light line with the fjord V
- Voltrieve Pilot 3
- 22 kW onboard charger
- Satin aluminium roof rails
- Portrait central touchscreen
- 19 inch Birch wheels, Light wool blend interior

### Fjord Plus

Everything in Fjord Pure, plus:

- Power tailgate with hands-free opening
- Keyless entry
- 360 camera
- 20 inch Fern wheels, Light wool blend interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning

### Fjord Prime

Everything in Fjord Plus, plus:

- Head-up display
- Panoramic glass roof
- Adaptive high beam
- 20 inch Fern wheels, Nappa leather interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning
- Pilot Plus pack included: Voltrieve Pilot lane change assist and highway autopilot upgrade
- Sound pack included: 14 speaker Voltrieve Stage audio system

## Fjord financing example

### Financing in Sweden

A Fjord Plus RWD at 571 000 SEK costs **6 367 SEK per month** over 36 months with Voltrieve Financial Services.

| Fjord Plus RWD financing in Sweden | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash price | 571 000 SEK |
| Deposit (20 %) | 114 200 SEK |
| Loan amount | 456 800 SEK |
| Monthly payment for 36 months | 6 367 SEK per month |
| Residual payment at the end (50 %) | 285 500 SEK |
| Nominal rate | 4.95 % |
| Effective rate | 5.33 % |
| Setup fee | 695 SEK |
| Total amount payable | 629 607 SEK |

Example in Sweden: cash price 571 000 SEK, deposit 114 200 SEK, loan 456 800 SEK over 36 months at a nominal rate of 4.95 % (effective rate 5.33 %). The monthly payment includes an administration fee of 59 SEK. A setup fee of 695 SEK is charged at the start, and a residual payment of 285 500 SEK is due at the end of the term, when you can pay it, refinance it or return the car. Total amount payable 629 607 SEK. Credit is subject to approval. The rate is variable and the example is for illustration only.
