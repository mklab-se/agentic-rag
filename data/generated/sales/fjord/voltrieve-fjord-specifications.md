## Fjord charging

| Fjord charging | Value |
|---|---|
| Peak DC charging power | 240 kW |
| DC charging from 10 to 80 % | about 20 minutes |
| Onboard AC charger | 22 kW |
| AC charging, empty to full | about 3.8 hours |
| Charge port | CCS2 |

The Fjord DC charging curve: the charge power the car accepts at each state of charge, at a charger that delivers at least 240 kW and with the battery at its working temperature.

| State of charge | Charge power |
|---|---|
| 10 % | 240 kW |
| 50 % | 200 kW |
| 80 % | 95 kW |
