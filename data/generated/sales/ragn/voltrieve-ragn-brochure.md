## Meet the Ragn

The Ragn is a crossover for people whose weekends start where the asphalt ends. Tall, tough and all wheel drive as standard, it takes the forest road as calmly as the motorway.

The Voltrieve Ragn is a crossover with all wheel drive, built on the Voltrieve V2 platform with an 800 V system. Built for the long way round: that is the idea behind every detail.

## Technology and Voltrieve Pilot

The Voltrieve Ragn comes with Voltrieve Pilot version 3, our driver assistance suite. It brings adaptive cruise, lane keeping, traffic jam assist and automatic lane change (with Pilot Plus).
Voltrieve Pilot Plus adds lane change assist and the highway autopilot. It is included on Ragn Prime and available as an option pack on the other trims.

- Ground clearance of 205 mm
- Off road mode
- 22 kW onboard charger
- A heat pump as standard, so the range holds up in winter

## Range and charging

With a 84 kWh battery the Voltrieve Ragn goes up to 530 km on a charge (WLTP). At a fast charger it takes up to 240 kW and goes from 10 to 80 % in about 21 minutes. At home, the 22 kW onboard charger fills the battery overnight.

| Battery | Range (WLTP) | Peak DC charging | 10 to 80 % | Onboard AC charger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 84 kWh | 530 km | 240 kW | 21 min | 22 kW |

## Questions and answers

**How far can the Ragn go on a charge?**
Up to 530 km (WLTP) with the 84 kWh battery. Cold weather, high speeds and a full load shorten that.

**How fast does it charge?**
From 10 to 80 % in about 21 minutes at a fast charger that delivers 240 kW or more. On AC it charges at up to 22 kW, empty to full in about 3.8 hours.

**Can it tow?**
Yes, up to 1 800 kg braked. The Tow pack adds an electrically deployable tow bar and trailer stability control.

**Does it have a heat pump?**
Yes, on every trim.

**Which driver assistance does it have?**
Voltrieve Pilot version 3: adaptive cruise, lane keeping, traffic jam assist and automatic lane change (with Pilot Plus).
