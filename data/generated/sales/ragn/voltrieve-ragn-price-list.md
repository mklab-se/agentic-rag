## What each Ragn trim adds

### Ragn Pure

- Heat pump
- Off road mode
- 22 kW onboard charger
- Voltrieve Pilot 3
- Square pixel LED lights
- Orange recovery hooks
- Black roof rails
- Washable textile seats
- 19 inch Trail wheels, Washable dark grey textile interior

### Ragn Plus

Everything in Ragn Pure, plus:

- Power tailgate
- Keyless entry
- 360 camera with underbody view
- 19 inch Trail wheels, Washable dark grey textile interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning

### Ragn Prime

Everything in Ragn Plus, plus:

- Heated windscreen
- Adaptive high beam
- 20 inch Trail wheels, Dark grey microsuede interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning
- Pilot Plus pack included: Voltrieve Pilot lane change assist and highway autopilot upgrade
- Sound pack included: 14 speaker Voltrieve Stage audio system

## Ragn financing example

### Financing in Sweden

A Ragn Plus AWD at 593 000 SEK costs **6 610 SEK per month** over 36 months with Voltrieve Financial Services.

| Ragn Plus AWD financing in Sweden | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash price | 593 000 SEK |
| Deposit (20 %) | 118 600 SEK |
| Loan amount | 474 400 SEK |
| Monthly payment for 36 months | 6 610 SEK per month |
| Residual payment at the end (50 %) | 296 500 SEK |
| Nominal rate | 4.95 % |
| Effective rate | 5.32 % |
| Setup fee | 695 SEK |
| Total amount payable | 653 755 SEK |

Example in Sweden: cash price 593 000 SEK, deposit 118 600 SEK, loan 474 400 SEK over 36 months at a nominal rate of 4.95 % (effective rate 5.32 %). The monthly payment includes an administration fee of 59 SEK. A setup fee of 695 SEK is charged at the start, and a residual payment of 296 500 SEK is due at the end of the term, when you can pay it, refinance it or return the car. Total amount payable 653 755 SEK. Credit is subject to approval. The rate is variable and the example is for illustration only.
