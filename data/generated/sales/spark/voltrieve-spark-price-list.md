# Voltrieve Spark price list

Prices valid from 1 November 2026. Recommended retail prices including VAT, in Sweden (SEK) and in the EU (EUR).

![The Voltrieve Spark](spark/hero.jpg)

## Spark prices per trim and drive

| Model | Drive | Price in Sweden | Price in the EU |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spark Pure | RWD | 289 000 SEK | 26 500 EUR |
| Spark Plus | RWD | 312 000 SEK | 28 600 EUR |
| Spark Prime | RWD | 335 000 SEK | 30 700 EUR |

The Voltrieve Spark has RWD as standard on every trim.

## What each Spark trim adds

### Spark Pure

- Round LED ring headlamps
- 10.25 inch touchscreen with navigation
- Voltrieve Pilot 2 with adaptive cruise control
- Heated front seats
- Automatic headlights
- Wireless phone charging
- 17 inch Pebble wheels, Light grey textile interior

### Spark Plus

Everything in Spark Pure, plus:

- Rear camera
- Keyless entry
- Privacy glass
- Automatic climate control
- 17 inch Pebble wheels, Light grey textile interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning

### Spark Prime

Everything in Spark Plus, plus:

- Panoramic glass roof
- Heated windscreen
- 17 inch Pebble wheels, Light grey textile and microsuede interior
- Winter pack included: heat pump, heated steering wheel and rear seats, battery preconditioning
- Sound pack included: 14 speaker Voltrieve Stage audio system

## Spark financing example

### Financing in Sweden

A Spark Plus RWD at 312 000 SEK costs **3 506 SEK per month** over 36 months with Voltrieve Financial Services.

| Spark Plus RWD financing in Sweden | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash price | 312 000 SEK |
| Deposit (20 %) | 62 400 SEK |
| Loan amount | 249 600 SEK |
| Monthly payment for 36 months | 3 506 SEK per month |
| Residual payment at the end (50 %) | 156 000 SEK |
| Nominal rate | 4.95 % |
| Effective rate | 5.55 % |
| Setup fee | 695 SEK |
| Total amount payable | 345 311 SEK |

Example in Sweden: cash price 312 000 SEK, deposit 62 400 SEK, loan 249 600 SEK over 36 months at a nominal rate of 4.95 % (effective rate 5.55 %). The monthly payment includes an administration fee of 59 SEK. A setup fee of 695 SEK is charged at the start, and a residual payment of 156 000 SEK is due at the end of the term, when you can pay it, refinance it or return the car. Total amount payable 345 311 SEK. Credit is subject to approval. The rate is variable and the example is for illustration only.
