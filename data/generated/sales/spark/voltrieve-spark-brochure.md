## Meet the Spark

The Spark is the small car that makes the city feel bigger. It slips into gaps other cars drive past, turns on the spot and still seats four adults and their shopping.

The Voltrieve Spark is a city car with rear wheel drive, built on the Voltrieve V1 platform with a 400 V system. Small outside, generous inside: that is the idea behind every detail.

## Technology and Voltrieve Pilot

The Voltrieve Spark comes with Voltrieve Pilot version 2, our driver assistance suite. It brings adaptive cruise and lane keeping.

- Turning circle of 9.8 m
- 2 ISOFIX seats
- One pedal driving
- A heat pump with the Winter pack, standard on Spark Plus and Prime

## Range and charging

With a 42 kWh battery the Voltrieve Spark goes up to 310 km on a charge (WLTP). At a fast charger it takes up to 95 kW and goes from 10 to 80 % in about 25 minutes. At home, the 11 kW onboard charger fills the battery overnight.

| Battery | Range (WLTP) | Peak DC charging | 10 to 80 % | Onboard AC charger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42 kWh | 310 km | 95 kW | 25 min | 11 kW |

## Questions and answers

**How far can the Spark go on a charge?**
Up to 310 km (WLTP) with the 42 kWh battery. Cold weather, high speeds and a full load shorten that.

**How fast does it charge?**
From 10 to 80 % in about 25 minutes at a fast charger that delivers 95 kW or more. On AC it charges at up to 11 kW, empty to full in about 3.8 hours.

**Can it tow?**
No. The Voltrieve Spark is not rated for towing.

**Does it have a heat pump?**
On Spark Plus and Prime, through the Winter pack. On Spark Pure the Winter pack is an option.

**Which driver assistance does it have?**
Voltrieve Pilot version 2: adaptive cruise and lane keeping.
